Privacy Policy.

How this website handles information.

Information entered in forms

Membership and event preview forms do not submit information to a server or create accounts. Contact form details are placed into an email draft on your device; they are sent only if you choose to press Send in your email app.

Discussion accounts and posts

The Discussions page uses Supabase for member sign-in, discussion posts, replies, and display names. Discussion content and member display names are visible to website visitors. Email addresses and passwords are handled by Supabase Authentication and are not displayed in discussions. Members must confirm their email if email confirmation is enabled for the project.

External services

Images, web fonts, and linked external resources may be requested from their providers when you view the site. The discussion board also connects to the configured Supabase project. Their privacy practices are governed by those providers.
