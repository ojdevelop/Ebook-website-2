Featured SelectionLiterary Fiction

The Midnight Library

Matt Haig

Between life and death there is a library, and within that library, the shelves go on forever. Every book offers a chance to try another life you could have lived. A moving story about choices, regrets, hope, and discovering what makes life meaningful.
