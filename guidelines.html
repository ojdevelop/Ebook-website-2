Community

Community Guidelines.

A thoughtful reading community begins with curiosity, care, and respect.

Be respectful and kind

Welcome different perspectives. Discuss ideas without personal attacks, discrimination, or harassment.

Engage thoughtfully

  • Stay relevant to the book or topic.
  • Explain your perspective and invite conversation.
  • Use spoiler warnings when discussing plot details.

Respect privacy and creative work

Do not share private contact information or reproduce material without permission. Credit ideas and sources when you refer to them.

Keep the space welcoming

Harassment, hate speech, spam, impersonation, and deliberately disruptive behavior do not belong in a literary community.
